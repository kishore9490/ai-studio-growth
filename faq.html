Understanding Our Advisory Service

We guide you through structured borrowing options. Our goal is to bring clarity and objective evaluation to complex institutional requirements.

What is CJ Consulting's role?

We are an independent consultancy providing tailored financial solutions and guidance. We prepare and present your credit case to financial institutions, but we are not a direct lender.

What types of financial solutions do you help with?

We help customers explore personal loans, vehicle finance, used car loans, education loans, home loans, business finance, MSME finance, working capital, OD/CC facilities, loan against property, mortgage finance, project finance, agriculture finance, loan against securities and government-supported schemes.

Do you provide business loans?

We help eligible businesses explore secured and unsecured business-financing options, working capital, OD/CC facilities and project finance.

Do you help with MSME loans?

Yes. We help eligible MSMEs and businesses explore relevant financing options based on their requirements and applicable eligibility conditions.

Do you help with government schemes?

Yes. We help customers explore applicable government-supported schemes such as CGTMSE, MUDRA, NLM, AIF, CGFMU and PMFME.

Eligibility and scheme benefits are subject to the applicable guidelines and requirements of the respective financial institution.

Is loan approval guaranteed?

No. The final financing decision is made by the respective financial institution based on its eligibility criteria, policies, documentation and other applicable requirements.

Need tailored advice on your specific financial situation?

Our consultancy-led guidance is designed to address your unique requirements.
