Government Schemes

Government-Supported
Financial Schemes

Navigating government-backed funding requires clarity on guidelines. We assist micro, small, and medium enterprises in presenting structured project reports to lenders.

Check Scheme Options
Supported Programs
  • Explore Applicable Government-Supported Schemes
  • Government-supported schemes can provide different forms of financial or credit support for eligible individuals, entrepreneurs, MSMEs, agricultural activities and other qualifying requirements.
  • Understanding which scheme may be relevant can sometimes be difficult because each scheme has its own eligibility conditions, guidelines and requirements.
  • CJ Consulting & Financial Services helps customers in Mysuru and across Karnataka explore applicable government-supported financial schemes based on their requirements.

CGTMSE

  • Credit Guarantee Support for Eligible MSEs
  • CGTMSE-related support may help eligible micro and small enterprises access credit through applicable credit-guarantee mechanisms.
  • Eligibility and financing remain subject to the applicable scheme guidelines and requirements of the respective financial institution.

MUDRA

  • Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana
  • MUDRA supports eligible micro enterprises and businesses through applicable financing categories.
  • We help customers understand the scheme and explore relevant financing possibilities based on their business requirements and eligibility.

NLM

  • National Livestock Mission
  • Eligible applicants involved in applicable livestock-related activities may explore financing opportunities under relevant National Livestock Mission provisions.
  • Scheme conditions and eligibility requirements apply.

AIF

  • The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund supports eligible infrastructure-related projects within the applicable framework.
  • We help eligible applicants explore whether the scheme may be relevant to their project and financing requirement.

CGFMU

  • Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units
  • Eligible financing requirements may be supported through applicable credit-guarantee mechanisms under CGFMU.
  • Eligibility and availability are subject to the applicable scheme guidelines and financial institution requirements.

PMFME

  • PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises
  • PMFME is designed to support eligible micro food-processing enterprises under its applicable framework.
  • We help eligible applicants understand the scheme and explore relevant financing possibilities.
