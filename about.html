About CJ Consulting &
Financial Services

CJ Consulting & Financial Services is a Mysuru-based financial solutions consultancy helping individuals, professionals, entrepreneurs and businesses explore suitable financing options based on their requirements.

Our approach is shaped by practical banking experience, an understanding of credit and risk, and a focus on helping customers navigate the financing process with greater clarity.

Our Approach

The experience behind CJ Consulting & Financial Services helps us look at financing requirements from more than one perspective.

We aim to understand:

  • What does the customer need?
  • What is the purpose of the financing?
  • What financing options may be relevant?
  • What eligibility and documentation requirements apply?

From there, we help customers explore suitable financial solutions through our banking and financial-service network.

Our Guiding Principles

From Banking Experience to Financial Consultancy

CJ Consulting & Financial Services was built around a simple idea:

Financial decisions should be easier to understand.

Whether an individual is exploring a personal, vehicle or home-financing requirement, or a business is looking for working capital, business finance or property-backed funding, every requirement is different.

Our role is to understand that requirement and help customers navigate the available financing possibilities with greater clarity.
